# Product

## Register

product

## Users

International Medical Graduates (IMGs) preparing for the AMC MCQ Part 1 exam. They study in high-stakes, self-directed mode — time-pressured, reviewing hundreds of questions, tracking their own weaknesses without institutional support. They are professionals, not students: they've already completed medical training and are navigating a foreign credentialing system. When they open this app, they are in focused work mode.

## Product Purpose

AMC AI Coach is a study performance tool that helps IMGs track study sessions, log and analyse mistakes, identify weak topic areas, and receive AI-generated coaching on what to prioritise next. The AI synthesises study data into specific, actionable recommendations — not generic encouragement. Success looks like a user who opens the app, immediately knows where their gaps are, and has a clear plan for the session ahead.

## Brand Personality

Sharp · Focused · Trustworthy

The product communicates with expert confidence. It earns trust through precision — specific data, specific recommendations, no hedging. The tone is that of a highly competent study partner who respects the user's intelligence and time. Not cold, but never soft.

Reference: Linear — the density, keyboard-first feel, and the sense that every pixel is doing work. A tool built for experts that feels fast and deliberate.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS startup aesthetic** — no purple gradients, hero metrics, "Sign up for free" energy, or CMO-approved color palettes. This is a tool, not a product marketing page.
- **Clinical / hospital software** — no grey-on-grey, clunky form-heavy EMR aesthetics. The subject matter is medicine; the software doesn't need to look like it runs on a hospital server.
- **Gamified study apps** — no streaks-as-identity, bright reward animations, or Duolingo-style encouragement theater. The exam is serious; the app reflects that.

## Design Principles

1. **Expert tool, not consumer app** — IMGs are credentialed professionals. The UI treats them as capable of handling density, specificity, and information. No excessive hand-holding, no over-simplified UX, no empty-state cheerfulness.
2. **Data serves judgment** — Every metric, chart, and AI insight exists to help the user decide what to study next. If a data point doesn't answer "so what do I do?", it doesn't earn screen space.
3. **Precision over warmth** — The AI coach's credibility comes from accuracy and specificity. A recommendation that names the exact topic and explains the gap is worth more than three sentences of encouragement.
4. **Speed as respect** — Navigation is instant, actions are direct, friction is minimal. The user's time budget is finite and the exam date is fixed.
5. **Confidence through consistency** — A student under exam pressure doesn't need surprises in their tools. Predictable patterns, reliable states, and clear feedback build the ambient trust that lets them focus on studying.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline. Reasonable accessible choices throughout: sufficient contrast ratios, keyboard navigability, screen reader labels on interactive elements. No formal audit required at this stage.
